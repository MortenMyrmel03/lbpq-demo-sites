Electrical Services in Madison, WI

Lakeside Electric offers complete residential and light commercial electrical services across Madison and Dane County. All work is performed by Wisconsin-licensed electricians and inspected to code.

Electrical panel upgrades

Older Madison homes often run on 60- or 100-amp service that cannot keep up with modern kitchens, heat pumps and EV chargers. We replace outdated panels and fuse boxes with 200-amp service, coordinate with the utility and handle the permit.

EV charger installation

We install Level 2 home chargers for every make of electric vehicle, size the circuit correctly and register the installation for available rebates.

Rewiring and electrical repair

Aluminum wiring replacement, knob-and-tube removal, GFCI and AFCI protection, outlet and switch repair, and troubleshooting of flickering lights and tripping breakers.

Lighting and smart home

Recessed lighting, under-cabinet LEDs, landscape lighting, dimmers and smart switches installed and configured.
